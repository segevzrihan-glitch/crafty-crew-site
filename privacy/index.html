Crafty Crew Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Crafty Crew ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game made by Segev Zrihan Ziv. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Questions: craftycrew196@gmail.com, or Settings > Contact Us in the game.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name or email. The first time you open the game, it creates an anonymous account for your device so your progress is saved.
  • If you create an account, we keep your email and a username so you can log in on another device and add friends.
  • The game currently has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases. It does send us error and crash reports so we can fix bugs; they aren't linked to you or your account. We don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the game: Settings > Delete Account.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising identifier or payment details.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in system: accounts, progress, match results, friends, blocks, player reports, and error and crash reports. It also runs the small server functions that check match results and award rewards, and it sends the one-time email code.
  • Hetzner Online hosts our online match server in Germany. To start an online match, the game gets a one-time join ticket from Supabase and gives it to the server. The ticket carries your account ID, display name, level, whether you're a guest, your unlocked characters and the players you've blocked, and it expires after a minute. Your sign-in token is never sent to the match server. After the match, the server sends the results to Supabase.
  • Apple and Google distribute the game through their app stores, under their own privacy policies.

Other players can see your display name, username, level and selected character. Your friends can also see whether you're online. Anyone who opens the leaderboard sees the display name, level and XP of the top players.

If you tap INVITE ON WHATSAPP, the game opens WhatsApp with a message you can choose to send. We don't send anything to WhatsApp ourselves.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players or the game from abuse.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress, match results, friends and blocks are kept until you delete your account.
  • Reports are kept for review. If you delete your account, reports you sent stay, but without your ID. Reports about you are kept for 90 days after you delete your account, with only the name you had, the reason and the dates (no IDs), and are then deleted automatically.
  • Match server logs are limited in size and overwritten automatically as new ones are written.
  • Error and crash reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • If you uninstall the game without deleting your account, the data stays on our servers, but a guest account can no longer be reached from your device. Write to us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your account

In the game, go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm twice. This permanently deletes your account from our servers, together with your profile, progress, characters, match history, friends and requests, invites and blocks. Reports about you are kept as described above. It can't be undone. Your device then starts over as a new guest.

You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to us (Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID filled in), or at craftycrew196@gmail.com from the email address on your account. For a guest account, send your display name and roughly when you last played, so we can find it.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name in your profile, block or report other players, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we'll reply within one month. If you live in the EU, the UK or Israel, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we use your information because it's needed to provide the game you asked for (account, progress, matches, friends), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working (server logs, error and crash reports, blocks and reports).

Children

Crafty Crew isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

Accounts, progress, match results and error reports are sent to Supabase over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each player can read only their own private data. Online match traffic uses the game's own network protocol, which isn't encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

If we add advertising, analytics or other third-party tools in the future, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details in the store before that version is released.
