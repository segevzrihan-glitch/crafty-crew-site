Crafty Crew Support

Crafty Crew is a mobile game for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Need help?

If something isn't working, you lost progress, you want to report a player, or you have a question or idea, email us and we'll get back to you.

Email: craftycrew196@gmail.com

In the game, Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID already filled in, which helps us find your account. If you write from another app, please include your display name, your phone model and roughly when the problem happened.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings > Delete Account in the game, or email us and we'll delete it for you.
