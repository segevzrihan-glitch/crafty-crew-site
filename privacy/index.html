Crafty Crew Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Crafty Crew ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game made by Segev Zrihan Ziv. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Questions: craftycrew196@gmail.com, or Settings > Contact Us in the game.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name or email. The first time you open the game, it creates an anonymous guest account for your device straight away, while the sign-in screen is showing, so your progress is saved whether you sign in or play as a guest.
  • If you create an account, with your email or by signing in with Google or Apple, we keep your email and a username so you can sign in on another device and add friends.
  • The game currently has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases. It does send us error and crash reports so we can fix bugs; they aren't linked to you or your account. We don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the game: Settings > Delete Account.

To start an online match, the game gets a one-time join ticket from Supabase and gives it to the match server. The ticket carries your account ID, display name, level, whether you're a guest, your unlocked characters and the players you've blocked, and it expires after a minute. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The game currently has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools. This section explains what would change if we add them in the future, so you know what to expect. We will update this policy and the app's privacy details in the store before any such version is released.

What such tools could collect: device identifiers and advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID), in-app events (for example, matches played, screens opened and purchases), your approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level, never precise GPS location), and crash data.

Why: to measure how the game is played and improve it, and to show ads and measure how they perform.

Your consent: on iOS, the game will ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and will not track you if you decline. On Android, we will follow Google Play's policies, including its rules on the Advertising ID and on ads in games. Where the law requires your consent (for example, in the EU or the UK), we will ask for it first.

How to opt out: on iOS, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or tracking for Crafty Crew in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, delete or reset your Advertising ID, or turn off ads personalization, in your device's Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

We will never sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress, match results, friends and blocks are kept until you delete your account.
  • What we keep from Google or Apple (your email and account ID) stays with your account until you delete it. The Apple token is deleted when your account is deleted. If Apple can't be reached at that moment, we try again every day and delete the token as soon as Apple confirms.
  • Reports are kept for review. If you delete your account, reports you sent stay, but without your ID. Reports about you are kept for 90 days after you delete your account, with only the name you had, the reason and the dates (no IDs), and are then deleted automatically.
  • Match server logs are limited in size and overwritten automatically as new ones are written.
  • Error and crash reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • If you uninstall the game without deleting your account, the data stays on our servers, but a guest account can no longer be reached from your device. Write to us and we'll delete it.

Your rights / delete your data

In the game, go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm twice. This permanently deletes your account from our servers, together with your profile, progress, characters, match history, friends and requests, invites and blocks. Reports about you are kept as described above. It can't be undone. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also ends Crafty Crew's access to your Apple ID (you can check in your iPhone's Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple). If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the game's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your device then starts over as a new guest.

You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to us (Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID filled in), or at craftycrew196@gmail.com from the email address on your account. For a guest account, send your display name and roughly when you last played, so we can find it.

You can change your display name in your profile, block or report other players, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we'll reply within one month. If you live in the EU, the UK or Israel, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we use your information because it's needed to provide the game you asked for (account, progress, matches, friends), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working (server logs, error and crash reports, blocks and reports).

Children

Crafty Crew isn't directed at children under 13 and is rated 13+. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

Accounts, progress, match results and error reports are sent to Supabase over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each player can read only their own private data. Online match traffic uses the game's own network protocol, which isn't encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

If we add advertising, analytics or other third-party tools in the future, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details in the store before that version is released.
