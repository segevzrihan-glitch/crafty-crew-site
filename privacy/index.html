Crafty Crew Privacy Policy

Effective: [RELEASE DATE OF 1.2]
Previous version: 16 November 2026

Crafty Crew is a mobile game made by Segev Zrihan Ziv ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: craftycrew196@gmail.com, or Settings > Contact Us in the game.

1. Playing as a guest or with an account

You can play as a guest without giving a name or email: the first time you open the game it creates an anonymous guest account for your device. If you create an account (email, Google or Apple), you can sign in on other devices and keep your progress; if you were playing as a guest, your progress moves to that account.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: an anonymous guest ID created at first launch; if you register, your email, and your username, display name, friend code and avatar (a picture of one of the game's characters, never a photo). Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are not stored in readable form.
  • Sign-in providers: your Google or Apple account ID and email (Apple "Hide My Email" relay addresses are supported). Google also sends the name and photo on your Google account; we don't keep them. For Apple sign-in we also store a token used only to end the game's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account.
  • Gameplay: progress (level, XP, cans, gems, characters and items), match results, rewards, missions, friends and invites, blocks, reports and last-online time.
  • Rewarded ads: a record of each rewarded-ad reward (time, reward type and amount, the ad unit and the ad network's transaction ID). We use it to grant each reward only once and to enforce daily limits.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device, your notification choices (friend invites, news) and your time zone (as an offset from UTC), so we can send invites and updates at reasonable hours (never between 23:00 and 09:00 your time).
  • Technical: your IP address while connected, server logs of online matches (room codes, display names of players joining and leaving, start and end), and error and crash reports (the game's build, your platform and its version, the error and where it happened; names, email addresses and sign-in tokens are removed before sending, and reports don't include your account ID).
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase collects in-game events (for example tutorial progress, matches, level ups and shop visits) and device information (model, operating system, app version, language, approximate region from your IP address), linked only to a random ID for this installation, never to your name, email or account. Google Analytics doesn't collect your advertising ID, and Google Signals and ad personalisation are off. You can turn it off in Settings > Share Usage Data.
  • Advertising ID: the device advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID; on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), used by the ad and ad-measurement services described in section 4.
  • Purchases (iPhone): records of gem pack purchases from Apple (Apple's transaction IDs, the pack, the date, and refunds). We never see your card or payment details.
  • Moderation: records of moderation actions taken on your account.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Sound, music, vibration and aim-assist settings, favourite characters and tutorial progress stay on your device.

Other players can see your display name, username, avatar, level and selected character; your friends can also see whether you're online, and the leaderboard shows the display name, level and XP of the top players. If you tap INVITE ON WHATSAPP, the game opens WhatsApp with a message you can choose to send; we don't send anything to WhatsApp ourselves.

3. Who we share data with (service providers)

  • Supabase – backend, database, sign-in and error reports (EU, Frankfurt).
  • Hetzner – online match servers (Germany).
  • Google – Google sign-in; Firebase Analytics; Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications); Google AdMob (ads) and Google's User Messaging Platform (consent). On iPhone, Firebase also asks Apple for an Apple Search Ads attribution token, which only says whether the game was installed from an ad in the App Store and uses no advertising identifier.
  • Apple – Apple sign-in, in-app purchases and notifications on iPhone.
  • Meta (Facebook) – the Meta SDK (App Events) to measure our ad campaigns: app installs, opens and in-game events such as completing the tutorial, finishing a match, registering an account, reaching a level, unlocking a character, spending gems and verified purchases. We do not send your name, email, username, account ID or friend code to Meta.

Store distribution is handled by Apple and Google under their own policies. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players or the game from abuse. We never sell your personal data.

4. Ads, ad measurement and your choices

The game shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads you choose to watch (FREE CANS on Home, DOUBLE IT! after a match). We only grant the reward after Google confirms to our server that the ad was watched.
  • Interstitial ads occasionally when returning to Home after a match (never during a match, and not in your first matches).

AdMob may use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and approximate location (derived from your IP address) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Ads are limited to content rated for teens (maximum ad content rating T), and we block these ad categories: alcohol, gambling (18+), social casino games, dating, sexual references, and sexual and reproductive health.

iPhone: we ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) after you finish a match, never at launch. If you don't allow it, Apple doesn't give us or our partners the IDFA; you will still see ads, but they are less personalised, and campaign measurement uses Apple's privacy-preserving methods (SKAdNetwork / AEM). You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy or Google > Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).

Turning off analytics and measurement: Settings > Share Usage Data stops Google Analytics and the Meta SDK in the game (and resets the game's analytics ID on your device). It doesn't turn off ads.

Where required by law, we ask for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy Choices (shown only where it applies).

5. Notifications

Notifications are off until you allow them. You can turn friend invites and news on or off in the game (Settings > Notifications) or in your phone's settings.

6. How long we keep data

  • Analytics data: 14 months (deleted automatically by Google).
  • Error and crash reports: 30 days.
  • Rewarded-ad reward records: up to 12 months.
  • Push tokens: until you turn notifications off for the game in your phone's settings (removed the next time you open the game), delete your account, or the token stops working. We keep at most 5 devices per account.
  • Accounts, progress, match results, friends and blocks: until you delete your account. The Apple token is deleted with your account.
  • Reports: reports you sent stay without your ID; reports about you are kept for 90 days after you delete your account, without IDs. Our moderators' log of actions stays as our record, without your account ID.
  • Purchase records without an account ID: up to 7 years for accounting.
  • Match server logs are limited in size and overwritten automatically.
  • Ad networks and Meta keep data under their own policies.

7. Deleting your data and your rights

Settings > Delete Account (confirmed twice) permanently deletes your account and game data. You can also email us (Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID filled in). If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also ends the game's access to your Apple ID. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to us and we'll reply within one month.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn an account belongs to a child under 13 we will delete it. Parents can contact us to request deletion.

9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS to our backend, and each player can read only their own private data. Online match traffic uses the game's own real-time protocol and is not encrypted.

10. Changes

We post updates on this page with a new effective date, and tell players in the game about significant changes.
