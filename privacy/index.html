Crafty Crew Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Crafty Crew ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile game made by Segev Zrihan Ziv. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Questions: craftycrew196@gmail.com, or Settings > Contact Us in the game.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name or email. The first time you open the game, it creates an anonymous guest account for your device straight away, while the sign-in screen is showing, so your progress is saved whether you sign in or play as a guest.
  • If you create an account, with your email or by signing in with Google or Apple, we keep your email and a username so you can sign in on another device and add friends.
  • On iPhone you can buy gems with real money through Apple's In-App Purchase. Gems only buy looks (skins, turfs and tags), never an advantage in matches, and nothing you buy is random. Apple handles the payment: we never see your card or payment details.
  • The game has no ads. It uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, without your name or email, how the game is played (for example how far players get in the tutorial and how long matches last), and it sends us error and crash reports so we can fix bugs. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings > Share anonymous usage data. We don't use your advertising identifier, we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the game: Settings > Delete Account.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Analytics. The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the game is played (see "What we collect"). It identifies an installation of the game only by a random app instance ID, which isn't linked to your account, name or email. We have turned off Google Signals and ads personalization, the game doesn't collect your advertising identifier, and analytics data isn't used for advertising or shared with data brokers. Google keeps it for 14 months.

Apple Search Ads attribution (iPhone). Firebase uses Apple's AdServices framework to learn whether the game was installed after an Apple Search Ads ad in the App Store. Apple provides this without the advertising identifier (IDFA) and without tracking you across other companies' apps or websites, so the game doesn't show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Turn it off: Settings > Share anonymous usage data. When it's off, the game stops sending analytics and resets the app instance ID on your device. Turning it back on starts with a new one.

Where the game is available: Crafty Crew is currently offered in Israel, the United States and Canada. If we make it available in the EU or the UK, we will first add a consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform) that asks for your permission for analytics and, if the game has ads by then, for ads, before any of it starts.

Ads. The game has no ads today. If we add ads in a future version, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details in the store first, and ask for your permission where the law or the store requires it (for example Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS before any tracking).

We will never sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics data in Google Analytics is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
  • Bans and name locks are kept while the account exists and are deleted with it. Our moderators' log of the actions taken stays as our record of moderation decisions, without your account ID once the account is deleted.
  • Your account, progress, match results, friends and blocks are kept until you delete your account.
  • What we keep from Google or Apple (your email and account ID) stays with your account until you delete it. The Apple token is deleted when your account is deleted. If Apple can't be reached at that moment, we try again every day and delete the token as soon as Apple confirms.
  • Purchase records are kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, the gems are deleted with it, and the purchase records are kept without your account ID, because we need them to handle refunds and for our accounting records. Purchase records without an account ID are kept for up to 7 years for accounting and tax, then deleted.
  • If Apple refunds a purchase, we take back the gems from it. If some of them were already spent, the rest is shown in the shop as GEMS OWED and is paid off from gems you earn later; you can't spend gems until it's cleared. Items you already have stay yours.
  • Reports are kept for review. If you delete your account, reports you sent stay, but without your ID. Reports about you are kept for 90 days after you delete your account, with only the name you had, the reason and the dates (no IDs), and are then deleted automatically.
  • Match server logs are limited in size and overwritten automatically as new ones are written.
  • Error and crash reports are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • If you uninstall the game without deleting your account, the data stays on our servers, but a guest account can no longer be reached from your device. Write to us and we'll delete it.

Your rights / delete your data

In the game, go to Settings > Delete Account and confirm twice. This permanently deletes your account from our servers, together with your profile, progress, characters, match history, friends and requests, invites and blocks. Reports about you are kept as described above. It can't be undone. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also ends Crafty Crew's access to your Apple ID (you can check in your iPhone's Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple). If you signed in with Google, you can also remove the game's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your device then starts over as a new guest.

You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to us (Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID filled in), or at craftycrew196@gmail.com from the email address on your account. For a guest account, send your display name and roughly when you last played, so we can find it.

You can change your display name in your profile, block or report other players, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we'll reply within one month. If you live in the EU, the UK or Israel, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we use your information because it's needed to provide the game you asked for (account, progress, matches, friends), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working (server logs, error and crash reports, blocks and reports). Analytics is based on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game; you can object at any time by turning it off in Settings.

Children

Crafty Crew isn't directed at children under 13 and is intended for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

Accounts, progress, match results and error reports are sent to Supabase over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each player can read only their own private data. Online match traffic uses the game's own network protocol, which isn't encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

If we add advertising or other third-party tools in the future, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details in the store before that version is released.
